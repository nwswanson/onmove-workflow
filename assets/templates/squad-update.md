# Squad update template

Use only for a material change or useful periodic synthesis. Omit empty sections;
replace every bracketed instruction. Keep one concise aggregate post per squad/run
unless distinct historical events need separate effective dates. Resolve actual
parent, rich-text payload, date and optional state through the OnMove adapter.

## Current picture

[Squad label, work outcome and the most consequential change. Distinguish known
progress, attributed reports, interpretation and unknowns.]

## What changed

[Evidence-linked changes to scope, implementation, verification, rollout or outcome.
Keep source event time and the actual time of this observation distinct.]

## Attention and open loops

[Render relevant RISKCALLOUT, BLOCKER, CHECKIN, RETRO or DECISION headers with
stable ID, STATUS, SQUAD and WORK values. Describe impact, confirmed/unknown owner,
next action, evidence and agreed due date or proposed review trigger.]

## Questions

[QUESTION ID … STATUS waiting: the smallest question that changes a conclusion or
unblocks an action. Reuse the stored question ID.]

## Resolutions

[RESOLVED plus original tag and ID, supported closure reason and evidence. Absence
of recent mentions does not establish resolution.]

## Coverage

[COVERAGEGAP for partial/unavailable sources; state what was actually checked.
Separate delivery outlook from evidence visibility.]

## Receipt

[COMMIT plus stable alphanumeric marker. Record run_id, checkpoint_id,
predecessor_checkpoint_id, effective event date/time, recorded_at and source/
evidence references. Include a full snapshot only when useful independently.]

End the prepared narrative and final rich-text paragraph with the following line.
Compute the canonical digest from the contract, then replace PENDING with its 64
lowercase hexadecimal characters in both representations before reservation. This
line is always the final standalone paragraph; do not publish the placeholder.
