# Team oversight update template

Persist this post in the configured Team oversight Thread under the tracking Focus.
OnMove does not support a direct Focus update. The Focus Note carries the current
rollup and directory; this post supplies chronological team-level evidence.

## Current picture

[The most consequential cross-squad outcome, dependency or decision change.]

## Manager attention

[Small actionable set of decisions/check-ins: reason, confirmed owner or owner gap,
next action, and explicit agreed timing or proposed review point. Use stable IDs
and searchable RISKCALLOUT, BLOCKER, DECISION, CHECKIN or RETRO tags.]

## Squads and work

[Brief material changes, shared project/dependency IDs and source links. Distinguish
new evidence from carried-forward context. Avoid copying every local risk.]

## Questions and resolutions

[Deduplicated QUESTION records with links to original squad/question IDs; RESOLVED
records only with supporting decisions/evidence.]

## Coverage

[Each selected squad's committed checkpoint/as-of and persistence status. Name
skipped, paused, stale, partial or blocked coverage. A successful focus post does
not make every squad's observation current.]

## Receipt

[COMMIT plus stable alphanumeric marker; run_id, predecessor_checkpoint_id,
checkpoint_id, effective_date and recorded_at. Retain supporting squad checkpoint
and OnMove Update references.]
